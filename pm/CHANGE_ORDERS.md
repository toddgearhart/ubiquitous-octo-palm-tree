# Change Orders

No change orders have been issued.

A change order modifies an active directive after review or new evidence. It does not silently rewrite history.

## Entry format

### CO-NNN — Short title

Status: PROPOSED | ISSUED | COMPLETE | CANCELLED  
Date: YYYY-MM-DD  
Directive: PM-NNNN  
Requested by: role

#### Reason

Evidence or changed condition that requires the order.

#### Required change

A bounded statement of added, removed, or revised work.

#### Acceptance delta

The exact acceptance criteria added, removed, or changed.

#### Out of scope

Work that remains unauthorized.

#### Program Manager approval

Name/role, date, and decision reference.

#### Completion evidence

Branch, commit, tests, and review result.
